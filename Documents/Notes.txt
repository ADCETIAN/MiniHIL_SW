+------+-----+-----+-------------+------+
| SOF  | CMD | LEN | DATA (0-LEN)| EOF  |

+------+-----+-----+-------------+------+
| SOF  | RSP | LEN | DATA (0-LEN)| EOF  |

SOF = 0xAA
EOF = 0x55

Suppose GPIO number 15 doesn't exist.

Meaning

Byte	Meaning

which command failed (0x10)

Small protocol improvement

Instead of sending just RSP_OK, I recommend making every acknowledgment include the original command:

AA 01 01 <CMD> 55

For example:

GPIO ACK: AA 01 01 10 55
Relay ACK: AA 01 01 20 55
ADC Start ACK: AA 01 01 30 55

This allows the PC application to match acknowledgments to the commands it sent, which becomes especially important if multiple commands are in flight.
